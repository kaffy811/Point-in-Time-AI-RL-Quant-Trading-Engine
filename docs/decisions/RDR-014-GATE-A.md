# RDR-014-GATE-A — Real Historical Provider Readiness

**Status: BLOCKED / RESEARCH OWNER DECISION REQUIRED**

Gate A cannot begin breadth ingestion until one approved provider combination supplies historical PIT index membership, stable security identity, executable open prices, corporate-action semantics, exchange calendar, and filing/revision availability for 2010–2025. The current SEC offline adapter is intentionally conservative and unsuitable for proving historical availability or revision completeness. Current constituents plus backfilled prices are explicitly disallowed. This decision records a provider-readiness blocker and does not change any frozen research assumption.
